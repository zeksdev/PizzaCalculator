# Ball weight excludes yeast

Flour is derived as `balls × ballWeight / (1 + hydration + salt)`, and yeast is then added on top, so a batch of 6 × 280 g actually weighs ~1681 g, not 1680 g. We chose this over the "exact total" formula because it matches both the reference calculator and the author's source recipe (1000 g flour + 650 g water + 30 g salt = 6 × 280 g, with 1 g yeast extra), keeping flour at round, recognisable numbers. Do not "fix" this to include yeast in the total.
